THE LENS PERSPECTIVE

Tokens ≠ shares.

Robinhood Chain Stock Tokens provide economic exposure through tokenised debt securities. They do not confer ownership rights in the underlying company.

TRANSPARENCY BY DESIGN

Know what the numbers mean.

One identity. Two balances.

Assets are matched by chain ID 4663 and the issuer registry’s contract address. A ticker alone never establishes identity.

Token units = raw balance ÷ 10^decimals.
Share equivalents = token units × verified shares-per-token multiplier.

Balances, decimals, multiplier and oracle-pause state are read at one block. A mismatch between the registry and contract multiplier leaves share exposure unavailable.

Prices with context

Robinhood REST bid/ask prices are underlying-equity prices. We use their midpoint × share equivalents for an indicative USD valuation.

Chainlink token prices already include the multiplier; applying it again would overstate the value. The live integration currently uses REST only.

Quotes older than 120 seconds, invalid prices and paused oracles are excluded. This conservative freshness policy also applies outside market hours. There is no P&L without a verified cost basis. FX conversion is not enabled.

Availability is not eligibility

Underlying session, token restrictions, venue availability and user eligibility are separate states. A public wallet cannot verify residence, account eligibility or permissions.

No authoritative holiday calendar or venue integration is configured, so session and venue states remain unknown. An issuer halt or inactive status is shown as restricted. A successful read never implies the ability to trade.

Corporate actions

Splits, reverse splits and reinvested dividends change the on-chain multiplier. RWA Lens never mints, burns or synthetically adjusts your balance.

Issuer action records are explanatory. Pending multipliers are displayed separately. Merger, spin-off, rename and delisting events are shown only when returned by the issuer, without guessing their economics.

Historical replay utilities support corrected ratio records; live historical valuations are unavailable until archival balances, ratios and prices are integrated.
